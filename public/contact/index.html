Best time to call

We're usually free to chat 9am–12pm and 2pm–6pm, Monday to Saturday. Changeover days (Fridays and Mondays) are busiest, so mornings before 10am are best then.

Finding us

We're on the edge of Sherwood Forest, about 15 minutes' drive south of Worksop. Worksop is the nearest railway station; we can recommend local taxis.

We send exact directions, what3words and the key-safe code a week before you arrive. Sat navs can lead you up a farm track, so please follow our directions for the last mile.
