About The Hayloft

Climb the old stone steps to a vaulted studio under oak beams, with a log burner, a deep freestanding bath and a sun-trap patio that looks over the paddock towards the forest edge.

The Hayloft sits above the old cart shed, its oak trusses and lime-washed walls left just as they were. We've kept it simple: a big, comfortable bed, a sofa pulled up to the log burner, and a freestanding copper bath under the window, so you can watch the light fade over the trees.

There's a compact, well-equipped kitchen for proper breakfasts, and a private patio with a bistro table that catches the evening sun. It's the cottage couples come back to for anniversaries, proposals and quiet winter weekends.

Things guests always ask

Can I bring my dog?

Not to The Hayloft, sorry. It's kept pet-free for guests with allergies. Keeper's Cottage and The Old Granary both welcome dogs.

Where do I park?

There's one space on the gravel drive at the foot of the Hayloft steps. If you've two cars, there's room for a second in the yard.

Is there a hot tub?

Not at The Hayloft, but the freestanding bath is big enough for two.

Where's the nearest pub and shop?

There's a traditional village pub with a real fire about 5 minutes' drive away, and a village shop for milk and papers 4 minutes' drive away. A larger supermarket is about 15 minutes' drive away in Worksop.

Is there mobile signal?

Patchy indoors (one bar on most networks), better on the patio. The wifi is reliable, so wifi calling is the easy fix.

Are there stairs?

Yes, 14 external stone steps with a handrail up to the front door. Inside, it's all on one level.
